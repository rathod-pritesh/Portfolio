Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Overview

This privacy notice explains how information is handled when you visit the developer portfolio of Pritesh Rathod (hosted at priteshrathod.vercel.app) or communicate via its programmatic API endpoints.

Information Collection and Use

Contact Inquiries: When you send a message through the website contact form or the POST /api/contact endpoint, you voluntarily provide your name, email address, and message text. This information is used strictly to read, respond to, and follow up on your correspondence.

Hosting and Server Logs: As with standard web hosting on Vercel and backend services on Render, standard server request logs (such as client IP address, user-agent string, referring URL, and request timestamp) are temporarily recorded for site security, operational performance monitoring, and anti-abuse defense.

Data Sharing and Third Parties

Your personal contact information is never sold, rented, or traded to third parties. Contact submissions are securely stored within MongoDB Atlas databases and accessed exclusively by Pritesh Rathod for communication purposes.

Questions and Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy statement, please contact Pritesh Rathod at rathodpritesh0712@gmail.com.
